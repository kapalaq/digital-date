# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Two partners using separate browsers or devices during a private, synced date-night session. They are in a casual evening context and need the interface to feel intimate, clear, and easy to follow without losing shared progress.

## Product Purpose

Digital Date runs one linear real-time date flow: waiting room, lobby warmup, game selection, travel quiz and planning, synced yoga, shared goals, and a celebration recap. Success means both users stay synchronized, understand what to do next, and feel like the app is a polished shared ritual rather than a generic multiplayer demo.

## Brand Personality

Romantic, cinematic, playful. The tone should feel private and premium while staying readable and task-focused.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, flat utility-only forms, overly cute scrapbook styling, and inconsistent stage visuals that make the flow feel stitched from unrelated screens.

## Design Principles

- Keep the shared journey obvious: every stage should show progression, partner status, and the next action.
- Use romance as atmosphere, not clutter: glow, glass, and soft color should support focus.
- Preserve synchronization confidence: waiting, ready, locked, and disconnected states must be explicit.
- Make each stage distinct but related through the same tokens, typography, chrome, and control vocabulary.
- Favor direct interaction over explanation; controls should make the next step self-evident.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for readable contrast on dark and light stage backgrounds, visible focus states, reduced-motion fallbacks, and text labels alongside non-obvious icons. Do not rely on color alone for readiness or completion states.
